mFG hirsutism cutoffs in South Asian populations

What cutoffs and prevalence figures have been published for South Asian and diaspora women, how were they measured, and how do they sit against the conventional cutoff of 8 and the P-PUP ethnicity-specific cutoffs (none of which come from South Asia)?

Early draft. A few studies extracted, none yet verified by a person against the paper. The systematic database search has not been run. This is a map of what exists, not a pooled estimate, and not clinical guidance.
